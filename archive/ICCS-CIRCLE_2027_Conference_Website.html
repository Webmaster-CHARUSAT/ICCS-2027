About ICCS-CIRCLE 2027

An interdisciplinary scientific forum designed to connect disciplines, methods and ideas around major challenges in circular and sustainable development.

Our Vision

To foster scientific convergence across physical, chemical, biological, mathematical and computational sciences and create new pathways toward sustainable development.

Our Mission

To provide a platform for researchers, young scientists, students and industry experts to exchange knowledge, present discoveries and build meaningful collaborations.

Why Convergence?

Complex scientific and sustainability challenges increasingly require combined experimental, theoretical, mathematical and computational approaches rather than isolated disciplinary solutions.

Scientific Themes

Five interconnected tracks define the scientific scope of the conference.

01 · Functional Materials, Nanoscience & Fundamental Physics

Nanomaterials, advanced functional materials, high-energy and hadron physics, astrophysics and cosmology.

02 · Sustainable Chemistry & Molecular Design

Synthetic, supramolecular and materials chemistry, green chemistry, catalysis and molecular design.

03 · Biological Interfaces, Biotechnology & Bioeconomy

Microbial and environmental biotechnology, plant biotechnology, disease biology, biomaterials and circular bioeconomy.

04 · Mathematical Sciences & Intelligent Modeling

Fractional calculus, fractional-order systems, mathematical modeling, tribology, special functions and computational modeling.

05 · Convergence Science & Emerging Computational Frontiers

AI in science, data-driven discovery, quantum and advanced computing, interdisciplinary modeling and sustainable systems.
