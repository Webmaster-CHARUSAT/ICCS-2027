About

About the
Conference

ICCS-CIRCLE 2027 is an interdisciplinary scientific forum designed to connect disciplines, methods and ideas around major challenges in circular and sustainable development.

Our Vision. To foster scientific convergence across physical, chemical, biological, mathematical and computational sciences and create new pathways toward sustainable development.

Our Mission. To provide a platform for researchers, young scientists, students and industry experts to exchange knowledge, present discoveries and build meaningful collaborations.

Objectives. Promote interdisciplinary research across materials, biology, chemistry and mathematical modeling; showcase advances in functional materials and biological interfaces for sustainable applications; explore intelligent modeling in scientific discovery; and provide a platform for young researchers and academia–industry collaboration.

Why Convergence? Complex scientific and sustainability challenges increasingly require combined experimental, theoretical, mathematical and computational approaches rather than isolated disciplinary solutions.

Conference Highlights

Scientific Themes

Five interconnected tracks define the scientific scope of the conference.

01 Functional Materials, Nanoscience & Fundamental Physics Nanomaterials, advanced functional materials, high-energy and hadron physics, astrophysics and cosmology.
02 Sustainable Chemistry & Molecular Design Synthetic, supramolecular and materials chemistry, green chemistry, catalysis and molecular design.
03 Biological Interfaces, Biotechnology & Bioeconomy Microbial and environmental biotechnology, plant biotechnology, disease biology, biomaterials and circular bioeconomy.
04 Mathematical Sciences & Intelligent Modeling Fractional calculus, fractional-order systems, mathematical modeling, tribology, special functions and computational modeling.
05 Convergence Science & Emerging Computational Frontiers AI in science, data-driven discovery, quantum and advanced computing, interdisciplinary modeling and sustainable systems.

Call for Papers

Author & Submission Guidelines

All submissions for ICCS-CIRCLE 2027 are handled through Microsoft CMT. Submission is separate from conference registration and payment, which are not handled through CMT.

Who Can Submit

Researchers, faculty, scientists, research scholars and students are invited to submit original work in any of the five scientific themes:

  • Functional Materials, Nanoscience & Fundamental Physics
  • Sustainable Chemistry & Molecular Design
  • Biological Interfaces, Biotechnology & Bioeconomy
  • Mathematical Sciences & Intelligent Modeling
  • Convergence Science & Emerging Computational Frontiers

What to Submit

  • Abstract length: 250–300 words (recommended).
  • Presentation formats: Oral and Poster; Invited presentations where applicable.
  • Publication: Selected submissions will be published as a Book Chapter / Proceedings / Full Paper in SCOPUS-indexed journals, subject to peer review.

Details on file format, templates and review policy will be published here once confirmed.

Key Dates

  • Submission deadline: 5 December 2026
  • Notification of acceptance: 20 December 2026
  • Conference: 21–22 January 2027, PDPIAS, CHARUSAT, Changa, Gujarat, India
